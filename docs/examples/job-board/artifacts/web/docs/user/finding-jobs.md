# Finding jobs on Hireboard

You can browse every open job on Hireboard without an account. You only need to sign in when you apply.

## Search for jobs

Open **Jobs** in the header, or go to the home page, which takes you to the job list.

The newest jobs come first, 20 per page. Each card shows the job title, the company, the location, and the salary when the employer has published one.

To narrow the list, use the filters above it and press the search button:

| Filter | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Keyword | Finds jobs whose posting matches the words you type, for example `designer` or `laravel`. |
| Location | Finds jobs in a city, for example `Berlin`. |
| Remote only | Shows only jobs you can do remotely. |
| Type | Full-time, Part-time, Contract or Internship. |

Filters work together. If you tick **Remote only** and choose **Full-time** under **Type**, you see only jobs that are both remote and full-time.

Your filters are part of the page address, so you can bookmark a search or send it to a friend. Reloading the page keeps your filters.

If nothing matches, clear one of the filters and search again.

## Read a job

Click a job to open its page. You see the full description, the location, whether it is remote and the employment type.

Salaries are yearly amounts in the currency the employer chose, for example **€60,000 – €80,000 a year**. When a card shows no salary, the employer did not publish one.

To apply, click **Apply**. If you are not signed in, Hireboard asks you to sign in or create a candidate account first.

## Jobs that are no longer available

When an employer closes a job, it disappears from search and its page shows **not found**, even if you saved the link. This is expected: closed jobs no longer accept applications.

## Look at a company

Click the company name on a job page to open the company's careers page. It shows:

- what the company does, where it is based and a link to its website
- every job the company currently has open
- the company's average rating from candidate reviews and how many reviews it has, with a link to read them

Reviews are written by candidates and checked by the Hireboard team before they appear. Reviewers are always shown as "Verified candidate". A company with no published reviews shows no rating yet.

## Tips

- Run one search with **Location** and another with **Remote only** to see both local roles and roles you can do from anywhere.
- Bookmark searches you repeat, for example remote contract roles.
- Check the careers page before applying to see the company's other openings.
