Questions for validation (raised by the Criteria phase, not decided by the code) and Maya Chen's answers:

  • AC-2: filters combine with AND. Is that the intended behavior, or should q also match the company name?
    • Answer (Maya Chen, 2026-11-03): AND is intended. q matches the posting only; searching by company is what the company filter is for.
  • AC-3: a per_page above 50 is capped, not rejected with 422. Confirm that this is acceptable for API clients.
    • Answer (Maya Chen, 2026-11-03): capping is acceptable. No 422 for a large per_page.
  • AC-9: components/job-card.tsx already formats one-sided ranges as From … and Up to …, and the demo seed has one (QA Engineer at Northwind Health, maximum only). Confirm that wording is the intended display.
    • Answer (Maya Chen, 2026-11-03): the wording is the intended display. Keep From €X a year and Up to €X a year.

Manual cases

QA-M01: only open postings, newest first (AC-1)

Files: app/jobs/page.tsx, components/job-card.tsx, JobPostingRepository.php

#ActionExpected
1Open http://localhost:3000/Redirects to /jobs.
2Look at the list.About 8 postings from Acme Robotics, Northwind Health and Blue Fern Studio. "Legacy PHP Maintainer" is not listed.
3curl -s 'http://localhost:8000/api/v1/jobs' | jq '[.data[] | {title, status, published_at}]'Every status is "open", published_at descending. The order matches the page.
4Search for Legacy in the keyword field and submit.No results. The closed posting never appears in search.

QA-M02: filters and the URL (AC-2, AC-4)

Files: components/job-filters.tsx, lib/search-params.ts, JobSearchDTO.php

#ActionExpected
1On /jobs, tick remote and submit.URL becomes /jobs?remote=1. curl -s 'http://localhost:8000/api/v1/jobs?remote=1' | jq '[.data[].remote] | unique' prints [true], and the page shows the same postings.
2Also choose employment type "Full time" and submit.URL contains remote=1&employment_type=full_time (order may differ). Results are a subset of step 1.
3Reload the page.Both filters stay selected and the results are unchanged: the URL is the state.
4Open /jobs?company=acme-robotics.Only Acme Robotics postings are listed.
5Open /jobs?remote=&employment_type=astronaut&foo=bar.The page renders the unfiltered list. No error is shown and the form shows no selected filters.
6Open /jobs?company=does-not-exist.An empty result state, not an error page.

QA-M07: salary on job cards (AC-9)

Files: components/job-card.tsx

#ActionExpected
1curl -s 'http://localhost:8000/api/v1/jobs?per_page=50' | jq '[.data[] | {title, salary}]'Pick one posting with both bounds, one with a single bound and one with salary: null.
2Find the posting with a salary on /jobs.The card shows the range in the posting currency, for example €60,000 – €80,000 a year for {min: 60000, max: 80000, currency: "EUR"}.
3Find the posting with only max set (QA Engineer at Northwind Health, {min: null, max: 60000, currency: "EUR"}).The card reads Up to €60,000 a year. A posting with only min would read From €X a year.
4Find a posting with salary: null (Robotics Software Intern at Acme Robotics).The card shows no salary line at all, with no placeholder text.

Out of scope

  • Authenticated flows (applications, profiles, reviews submission and moderation) are covered by their own specs' manual tests.
  • Employer publishing and closing postings ship with HIRE-11.
  • Salary validation (salary_min <= salary_max) applies when postings are written, which is HIRE-11. The browse journey only reads salaries.
