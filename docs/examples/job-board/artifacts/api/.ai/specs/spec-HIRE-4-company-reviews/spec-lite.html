Company reviews with moderation

Summary

Candidates rate and review companies, and an admin moderates every review before anyone else sees it. Job seekers get employer ratings they can trust: one review per candidate per company, no anonymous drive-by posts, and nothing public until a human has read it. The work adds one table, six API endpoints, a public reviews page and an admin moderation queue.

Context

  • Problem: HIRE-4 ("Candidates can rate and review companies; reviews are moderated; one review per candidate per company"). Hireboard shows companies but gives job seekers no signal about what working there is like. The rules were agreed with Maya Chen at the start (BR-40..BR-43 in docs/context/business-rules.md) and have not been implemented yet.
  • User impact: visitors see an average rating and approved reviews per company. Candidates can share their experience without exposing their identity. Admins keep abusive or identifying content off the site.

Scope

In scope

  • company_reviews table, ReviewStatus enum, CompanyReview model and factory (schema doc first).
  • API: submit a review, read your own review, public list with average rating, admin queue (pending reviews only, oldest first, paginated, each with candidate_name and the company name and slug; non-admins get 403, see AC-10), approve, reject.
  • Web: /companies/[slug]/reviews (public list, ReviewForm for candidates, status card when the candidate already has a review) and /admin/reviews (moderation queue).
  • Contract docs: docs/api/api-contracts.md and web/lib/api/endpoints.ts.

Out of scope

  • Editing, deleting or resubmitting a review after submission or after a decision (BR-44).
  • Employer replies to reviews.
  • Moderation by employers, or any employer view of reviews beyond the public list.
  • Requiring an application to the company before reviewing (clarity gate Q2).
  • Rating fields on GET /api/v1/companies/{slug} (see MNC-1 and decisions.md D1).
  • Notifications to the candidate when a review is decided.

Must NOT change

  • MNC-1: The GET /api/v1/companies/{slug} response shape is unchanged (no review fields are added).
  • MNC-2: GET /api/v1/jobs and the /jobs page are unchanged.
  • MNC-3: The GET /api/v1/auth/me shape and role semantics are unchanged.

Acceptance criteria

  • AC-1: WHEN an authenticated candidate submits a review (rating 1-5, title 5-120 chars, body 20-2000 chars) for an existing company THE SYSTEM SHALL store it with status pending and respond 201 with status "pending".
  • AC-2: IF the candidate already has a review for that company in any status THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL respond 409 with code review_already_exists.
  • AC-3: IF the authenticated user is an employer or admin THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL respond 403 to a review submission.
  • AC-4: IF rating, title or body is invalid THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL respond 422 with field errors.
  • AC-5: WHEN a guest submits a review THE SYSTEM SHALL respond 401.
  • AC-6: WHEN anyone requests a company's reviews THE SYSTEM SHALL return only approved reviews, newest first, paginated, with author "Verified candidate" and no candidate id or name.
  • AC-7: THE SYSTEM SHALL return meta.average_rating as the mean of approved ratings rounded half-up to 1 decimal, or null when there are none.
  • AC-8: WHEN an admin approves a pending review THE SYSTEM SHALL set status approved, moderated_by and moderated_at, and the review SHALL appear in the public list.
  • AC-9: WHEN an admin rejects a pending review with a 5-255 char reason THE SYSTEM SHALL set status rejected and store the reason. The review SHALL never appear publicly, and the candidate SHALL see status and reason via /reviews/mine.
  • AC-10: IF the review is not pending THEN approve and reject SHALL respond 409 review_already_moderated. IF the user is not an admin THEN they SHALL respond 403.
  • AC-11: WHEN a signed-in candidate without a review opens /companies/{slug}/reviews THE page SHALL show ReviewForm, and after submitting it SHALL show "Thanks - your review is awaiting moderation." With an existing review, the page SHALL show its status instead of the form (manual-test.md#step-3, #step-4).

Boundaries

  • Always: a Pest feature test per AC; service-repository with DTOs (INV-02); update docs/api/api-contracts.md and web/lib/api/endpoints.ts (INV-06); responses in the INV-01 envelope; error codes from the contract list.
  • Ask first: any change to the companies or users tables; a new dependency; any change to the company show payload.
  • Never: expose candidate identity publicly; weaken or delete existing tests; touch auth flows (registration, login, tokens, middleware).

    • web: lib/api/client.ts (apiFetch, ApiError), lib/session.ts (getCurrentUser, requireUser, getSessionToken), app/(account)/layout.tsx (role nav already links admins to /admin/reviews), middleware.ts (matcher already covers /admin/:path*).
  • Already implemented (do not rebuild):

    • Roles, Sanctum tokens and the seeded admin admin@hireboard.test (HIRE-3).

Decisions

See decisions.md: D1 rating summary in list meta, D2 status column, D3 unique index plus pre-check.

Open questions

  • Q1: Can a candidate edit or resubmit a review after it is rejected? — Accepted assumption: no. Moderation is final in v1, and the one-review rule (AC-2) applies whatever the status. Recorded as BR-44 in docs/context/business-rules.md.
  • Q2: Must the candidate have applied to the company before reviewing it? — Answered (Maya Chen): no, not for v1. Any candidate may review any company. Added to Out of scope.
  • Q3: What does "anonymous" mean for the public list and for admins? — Answered (Maya Chen): the public author is always "Verified candidate", with no candidate id or name. Admins see candidate_name in the queue. Folded into AC-6.
  • Q4: How is the average rounded, and what is shown with no reviews? — Answered (Maya Chen): one decimal, half-up, and null when there are no approved reviews. Folded into AC-7.
  • Q5: Do pending or rejected reviews count toward the average or the total? — Answered (Maya Chen): no, approved only. Folded into AC-6 and AC-7.
  • Q6: Who creates admin accounts? — Accepted assumption: admins are seeded only (DemoUserSeeder), never self-registered (BR-01). No admin management UI in this spec.
