Manual test — spec-HIRE-4-company-reviews

Verifies company reviews and moderation by hand. Every step lists the exact action and the exact expected result. Steps 3 and 4 are the only proof for AC-11; the rest back up the Pest suite.

Preconditions

  1. Both repos on feature/HIRE-4-company-reviews.
  2. Fresh local data (local only, this wipes the database):
    cd ~/work/hireboard/api && php artisan migrate:fresh --seed
  3. API running: php artisan serve --port=8000 in api/.
  4. Web running: npm run dev in web/, with API_BASE_URL=http://localhost:8000 in web/.env.local.
  5. Demo accounts (password password): candidate@hireboard.test, employer@acme.test, admin@hireboard.test.
  6. Use a private browser window so no earlier session cookie is present.

Step 1

No public reviews yet.

curl -s http://localhost:8000/api/v1/companies/acme-robotics/reviews | jq '{data, meta}'

Expected: "data": [], meta.total is 0, meta.average_rating is null.

Step 2

Guests see a sign-in prompt.

Open http://localhost:3000/companies/acme-robotics/reviews. Expected: heading "Reviews", the text "No published reviews yet." and a "Sign in as a candidate" link to /login?next=%2Fcompanies%2Facme-robotics%2Freviews. No form.

Step 3

A candidate submits a review (AC-11, AC-1).

  1. Sign in at http://localhost:3000/login as candidate@hireboard.test / password.
  2. Open http://localhost:3000/companies/acme-robotics/reviews.
  3. Expected: the form "Review Acme Robotics" with Rating (1-5), Title and Your review.
  4. Pick rating 4, title Clear goals, kind team, review Interviews were well organised and the feedback arrived within a week. and press "Submit review".
  5. Expected: the form is replaced by "Thanks - your review is awaiting moderation."

Step 4

The status card replaces the form (AC-11, AC-2).

Reload http://localhost:3000/companies/acme-robotics/reviews. Expected: a "Your review" card with "Your review is awaiting moderation." and the title Clear goals, kind team. No form. The public list is still empty.

Step 5

Validation messages (AC-4).

Sign in as a new candidate: register at http://localhost:3000/register with role Candidate, name Riley Reviewer, email riley@hireboard.test, password password123. Open http://localhost:3000/companies/northwind-health/reviews, enter title Meh and review Too short., submit. Expected: "Please fix the highlighted fields." plus a message under Title and under Your review. Nothing is stored.

Step 6

An admin approves (AC-8, AC-7, AC-6).

  1. Sign out (account menu, "Sign out"), sign in as admin@hireboard.test.
  2. Open http://localhost:3000/admin/reviews.
  3. Expected: "1 pending, oldest first." and the review by Casey Candidate for Acme Robotics.
  4. Press "Approve". Expected: the queue shows "Nothing to moderate."
  5. Open http://localhost:3000/companies/acme-robotics/reviews. Expected: the review is listed with "Verified candidate", the average shows 4.0 from 1 review, and the name "Casey Candidate" appears nowhere on the page.

Step 7

An admin rejects; the candidate sees the reason (AC-9, AC-10).

  1. As riley@hireboard.test, submit a valid review for Northwind Health (rating 2, title Slow hiring process, review Three weeks between interviews and no feedback at all.).
  2. As admin@hireboard.test on http://localhost:3000/admin/reviews, type the reason Please remove the recruiter's name. and press "Reject". Expected: the review leaves the queue.
  3. As riley@hireboard.test, open http://localhost:3000/companies/northwind-health/reviews. Expected: "Your review was not published." and "Reason: Please remove the recruiter's name.". The public list is empty.
  4. Try to decide it again:
    TOKEN=$(curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8000/api/v1/auth/login -H 'Accept: application/json' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"email":"admin@hireboard.test","password":"password"}' | jq -r .data.token)
ID=$(sqlite3 database/database.sqlite "select id from company_reviews where status='rejected' limit 1")
curl -s -X POST "http://localhost:8000/api/v1/admin/reviews/$ID/approve" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Accept: application/json' | jq '{status, code}'

    Run from api/. Expected: {"status": 409, "code": "review_already_moderated"}.

Step 8

Company show payload unchanged (MNC-1).

curl -s http://localhost:8000/api/v1/companies/acme-robotics | jq '.data | keys'

Expected exactly: ["description", "id", "location", "name", "open_jobs_count", "slug", "website"].

Step 9

Job search unchanged (MNC-2).

Open http://localhost:3000/jobs as a guest and filter by Remote. Expected: the same open postings, order and filters as before this branch; no review data on job cards.

Step 10

Employers and admins get no form (AC-3).

Sign in as employer@acme.test and open http://localhost:3000/companies/acme-robotics/reviews. Expected: the public list only. No form, no status card.

Rollback

The change adds one table and no data migration. To back it out locally: php artisan migrate:rollback --step=1 in api/ drops company_reviews; then revert the two branches.
