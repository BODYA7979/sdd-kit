companies

A hiring company. Public through its careers page and as the company block of every job posting. Written before the migration 2026_10_19_100000_create_companies_table (HIRE-1).

Model: App\Models\Company (HasUuids, HasFactory).

Derived fields

  • open_jobs_count in the public Company shape is not a column. CompanyRepository::findBySlug() computes it with withCount() over postings with status open (BR-10).
  • The average rating is not stored on the company. It is computed from approved reviews and returned in the reviews list meta (BR-43, HIRE-4 decision D1).
