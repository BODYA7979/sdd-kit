Hireboard product brief

Owner: Maya Chen (product). Status: draft business rules, to be validated by Maya Chen before they move to api/docs/context/business-rules.md.

Problem

Small companies hire a few people a year. Big job boards bury them, charge per posting and fill their inboxes with low-effort applications. Candidates, in turn, can't tell a good employer from a bad one before they apply.

Hireboard is a small, focused job board: open postings are easy to search, every company has a careers page, candidates apply with one profile, and company reviews are moderated so the ratings can be trusted.

Roles

RoleWhoCan
VisitorAnyone, not signed inSearch open postings with filters, open a posting, view a company's careers page and its approved reviews
candidateJob seeker, self-registeredEverything a visitor can, plus keep a private profile, apply to open postings, see their own applications, review a company once
employerHiring manager, self-registered together with their companyEverything a visitor can, plus see applications to their own company's postings
adminHireboard staff, seeded onlyApprove or reject company reviews

MVP scope

  1. Job search. Keyword, location, remote, employment type and company filters over open postings, newest first, paginated. Job detail shows the hiring company.
  2. Careers pages. A public page per company: name, location, website, description, open jobs and the approved-review rating.
  3. Accounts. Candidate and employer registration, login, logout. Employer registration creates the company.
  4. Candidate profiles. Headline, location, bio, skills, years of experience, open to work.
  5. Applications. A candidate applies to an open posting with an optional cover letter. Candidates see their applications; employers see applications to their postings.
  6. Moderated reviews. Candidates rate and review companies; admins approve or reject each review before it is public.

Non-goals (v1)

  • Payments, paid postings, invoices.
  • Messaging between candidates and employers.
  • Email verification, password reset emails, notifications of any kind.
  • Employers creating, publishing or closing postings in the UI. Postings are seeded until HIRE-11.
  • CV/file uploads, application status workflow (shortlist, reject), saved searches.

Business rules (draft)

IDs are stable: specs cite them, and gaps are numbered so related rules stay together.

Accounts

  • BR-01 Self-registration roles are candidate or employer. Requesting admin returns 422.
  • BR-02 Employer registration creates exactly one company owned by that employer. The slug is Str::slug(company_name), with -2, -3 ... appended when taken.
  • BR-03 Emails are unique, compared and stored lowercase.
  • BR-04 API tokens expire after 7 days. Logout revokes the current token.

Job postings

  • BR-10 Only open postings appear in search and detail. Draft and closed postings are never public (404 job_not_found).
  • BR-11 Search returns newest published first. per_page is 1-50, default 20.
  • BR-12 Salary is optional, annual, in whole currency units with an ISO currency. When present, salary_min <= salary_max.

Candidate profiles

  • BR-20 One profile per candidate. Only candidates have profiles.
  • BR-21 A profile is private to its owner. Employers see only name, email and headline, and only through applications to their own jobs.

Applications

  • BR-30 A candidate needs a profile before applying (422 profile_required).
  • BR-31 Only open postings accept applications (422 job_not_open).
  • BR-32 One application per candidate per posting (409 already_applied), also under a concurrent duplicate submit.
  • BR-33 Employers see applications only for their own company's postings, including closed ones.

Company reviews

  • BR-40 Only candidates can review a company. Each candidate gets one review per company, whatever its status (409 review_already_exists).
  • BR-41 New reviews are pending. Only admins approve or reject them. Rejecting requires a reason of 5-255 characters.
  • BR-42 Only approved reviews are public. The author is always shown as "Verified candidate"; admins see the candidate's name.
  • BR-43 Company rating is the mean of approved ratings, rounded half-up to 1 decimal, or null when there are none.
  • BR-44 Moderation is final in v1: no edit or resubmission after a decision. Source: the HIRE-4 clarity gate (accepted assumption, confirmed by Maya Chen).

Open questions for Maya Chen

  • Should a candidate have applied to a company before reviewing it? (Proposed: no for v1.)
  • Token lifetime: is 7 days right? Confirm with the auth decision (HIRE-2).

Success for the MVP

  • A visitor finds a relevant open posting in under a minute with filters.
  • A candidate goes from registration to a submitted application in one sitting.
  • No unmoderated review and no candidate identity is ever visible publicly.
